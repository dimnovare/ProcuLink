## Status legend

- **cold** — no contact attempted yet
- **contacted** — outreach sent, awaiting reply
- **replied** — they responded; conversation open
- **demo-booked** — calendar slot confirmed
- **pilot** — 14-day Pilot started (tenant created, first PO uploaded)
- **paid** — converted to Growth/Operations/Integration
- **lost** — declined or went dark for 30+ days after demo

---

## How to use this list

Work warm intros first — they convert 5-10x cold outreach. Send 5 cold touches/day Tue-Thu, never Mon/Fri. Target 3 demos/week, which means ~15 booked touches/week and ~75 cold touches/week (rows 11-50 plus second/third-touch follow-ups on rows 1-10). Move a row's Status the same day you act; if you don't, the pipeline lies. Re-rank weekly by warm-intro reachability, ERP fit (Erply/Directo first — connectors already built), and order volume (target 100-500/mo — fits Growth/Operations pricing). Kill any row at 4 weeks cold-no-reply; archive in Notes, do not delete. Row count is the leading indicator; replies, demos, and pilots are lagging. Don't add row 51 until row 50 has a status.
