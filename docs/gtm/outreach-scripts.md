## Rules

- One CTA per message. Demo, or reply with a file. Never both.
- "You" not "we". The reader is the protagonist.
- Name the specific ERP and a specific pain. Generic = ignored.
- Tue/Thu sends only. Mon = inbox triage, Fri = checked out.
- 3 touches max per cold prospect (DM → email → email). After that, archive — not deleted.
- Never attach a deck. The deck is the live demo.
